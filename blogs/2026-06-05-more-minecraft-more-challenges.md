Twitch and Minecraft launched a three-week sponsored streaming campaign starting June 6th, offering Partners and Affiliates in the US, UK, and Germany the opportunity to earn up to $1,000 total by streaming Minecraft with Channel Skins active. Each week features different themes, deliverables, and viewer rewards, with a limited number of participating channels receiving front page shelf placement from June 6-26.
